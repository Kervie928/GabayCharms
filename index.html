What is GabayCharms?

A discreet, solar-powered keychain designed to protect teens and young adults from street dangers. It’s wearable tech that looks cool and works smart.

Key Safety Features

  • GPS Tracking: Real-time updates to trusted contacts
  • Pulse Monitoring: Sends alerts if distress is detected
  • Manual Emergency Button: Press 3x for instant SOS
  • LED Strobe Light: Visual distress signal
  • AI Voice Command: Hands-free panic activation
  • Connects to Authorities: Faster emergency response

User Experience Features

  • Companion app for setup and tracking
  • Alerts via SMS, email, or social media
  • Data is secured and shared only with trusted contacts

Design & Power

  • Stylish and youth-friendly look
  • Solar-powered with power-saving mode
  • 100% waterproof and durable

Who Needs This?

  • Students walking home at night
  • Commuters in unfamiliar areas
  • Parents concerned about their teens
  • Women, senior citizens, and solo travelers
